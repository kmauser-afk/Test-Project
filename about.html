About Us

Rooted in community since 1985

St. Mary’s Bank was founded on a simple idea: banking should strengthen the neighborhoods it serves. Four decades later, that idea still guides everything we do.

Our Values

What we stand for

🤝

Our History

Four decades of growth

  • 1985 — Founded Opened our first branch with a mission to serve working families.
  • 1998 — Community Lending Program Launched a local small-business lending fund to support neighborhood entrepreneurs.
  • 2010 — Online Banking Launch Introduced online banking and bill pay for members across the region.
  • 2018 — Mobile-First Banking Rebuilt our mobile app with mobile deposit, card controls, and budgeting tools.
  • Today — 60,000+ Members Fourteen branches strong, still locally owned and community focused.
